# musicdl 1.1.4

## English

- musicdl 1.1.4 improves exact title-only search results with multiple artists under the default `lossless_first` policy by keeping catalogue relevance ahead of audio quality. Upgrade to 1.1.4 to receive the improvement; `best_available` keeps its existing quality-based ordering.
- The quick-install manifest and deployment examples select the `1.1.4` image pair. The `1.1.3` pair remains available for rollback.

## 中文

- musicdl 1.1.4 改进默认 `lossless_first` 策略下多个歌手的精确歌名搜索结果，优先保留曲库相关度，再考虑音质。升级至 1.1.4 后可使用这项改进；`best_available` 继续保留原有的音质排序。
- 快速安装清单和部署示例改为使用 `1.1.4` 镜像对；`1.1.3` 镜像对仍可用于回滚。
