# musicdl 1.1.2

## English

- Under the lossless-first policy, a channel resolver or transport failure now lets downloads try another eligible channel instead of ending the request immediately.
- Downloads can now try an initial channel and up to eleven distinct alternatives. Already attempted channels are excluded, smaller pools stop when exhausted, and explicit lower limits remain supported. Requests whose channels all fail can take longer than before.
- The panel preserves terminal errors reached during fallback. Media validation, duration verification, policy denials, and the shared download admission limits remain enforced.
- The quick-install manifest and deployment examples now select the `1.1.2` image pair. The administrator login, source diagnostics, channel health display, and concurrency improvements from `1.1.1` are included; the `1.1.1` image pair remains available for rollback.

## 中文

- 在「无损优先」策略下，某个渠道的解析器或传输失败后，下载会继续尝试其他符合条件的渠道，避免立即结束请求。
- 下载现在最多尝试首个渠道加十一个不同的备用渠道。已尝试的渠道不会重复使用，候选池耗尽时停止，仍支持显式设置更低上限。所有渠道均失败时，请求耗时可能比之前更长。
- 面板会保留切换渠道后遇到的终止性错误；媒体校验、时长验证、策略拒绝和共享下载并发限制继续生效。
- 快速安装清单和部署示例现在选择 `1.1.2` 镜像对，包含 `1.1.1` 的管理员登录、音源诊断、渠道健康度显示与并发优化；`1.1.1` 镜像对仍可用于回滚。
